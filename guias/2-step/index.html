Quick guide · company accounts

Turn on 2-Step Verification

Google will require it on every Natural Trade and Global Forest email account. It takes about 5 minutes.

Friday, November 13. From that day on, anyone who has not turned it on will no longer be able to sign in to their email, Drive or Calendar.

What you need

  • Your computer, signed in to your company email.
  • Your phone.
  • The Google Authenticator app on your phone. It is free.
Download for iPhone Download for Android

Step by step

What you click is outlined in orange. The screens are illustrations, and Google's wording may differ slightly.

1

On your computer, open the 2-Step Verification page with your company account. If it asks for your password, enter it.

Open 2-Step Verification
2

Click Phone number and add your mobile number. Google texts you a code. Enter it and click Verify.

3

Go back and click Authenticator. Then click Set up authenticator. A QR code appears.

4

On your phone, open Google Authenticator, tap + and choose Scan a QR code. Point the camera at the code on your computer.

5

On your computer, click Next, enter the 6-digit code shown on your phone and click Verify.

6

Go back to the main page and click Turn on 2-Step Verification. The page should say it is on.

7

Click Backup codes and then Get backup codes. Print them or keep them somewhere safe outside your email. Each code works once if you lose your phone.

Done. If the page already says 2-Step Verification is on, there is nothing else to do.

What changes day to day

  • On your usual computer, Google asks for the code only the first time. Check Don't ask again on this device.
  • On a new computer or phone, Google asks for your password and then the Authenticator code or a prompt on your phone.
  • Some email apps, such as Outlook or iPhone Mail, may ask you to sign in again. Choose Sign in with Google and follow the steps.

Common problems

ProblemWhat to do
You see "You can't sign in. Your sign-in settings don't meet your organization's 2-Step Verification policy"You didn't turn it on in time, or text message is your only second step. Contact IT so they can help you sign in and add Google Authenticator.
I changed or lost my phoneSign in with one of your backup codes and set up Authenticator again on the new phone. If you have no codes, contact IT.
The text message doesn't arriveUse the Google Authenticator code. That is why it helps to have both methods.
The Authenticator code doesn't workMake sure your phone's time is set automatically, and enter the code before it changes.
Outlook or my email app keeps asking for my passwordRemove the account from the app and add it again with Sign in with Google.

No one at the company will ever ask for your codes or your password. If someone asks, don't share them and contact IT.
